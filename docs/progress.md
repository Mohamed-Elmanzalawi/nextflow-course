??? info "How progress is counted"
    - **Activity done:**
        - a question answered;
        - a self-check question rated;
        - every step of a "Mission steps" checklist ticked;
        - a checker script that has **passed**.
    - **Lesson complete:** every required activity in the lesson is done.
    - **First-try score:**
        - for graded questions, your **first** attempt counts;
        - for self-check questions, "Got it" counts.

      Retrying is encouraged, but it doesn't change the score.
    - **Concepts to review:** questions you got wrong on the first try, or rated *Partly* / *Missed*, grouped by concept, with links back to them.
